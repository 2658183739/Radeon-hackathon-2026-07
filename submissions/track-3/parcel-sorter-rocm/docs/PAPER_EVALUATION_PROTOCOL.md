# Paper-Grade Geometry Evaluation Protocol

## Status and scope

This document freezes the next evaluation before its episodes are executed. It
records observable evidence, hypotheses, alternatives, decisions, and stopping
rules. It is not a transcript of hidden chain-of-thought.

The current geometry-aware planner improved the matched
`large_narrow_carton` development set from 5/20 to 15/20 successes and reduced
force aborts from 8/20 to 4/20 on one Radeon GPU. Those episodes
(`7000000`--`7000019`) have already informed implementation choices and are not
eligible as unseen confirmatory evidence.

## Screening question

Does collision-checked reset and geometry-aware grasp planning improve a
balanced set of supported parcel profiles without hiding regressions in easy
boxes or cylinders?

The frozen exploratory campaign is
`configs/campaign_geometry_screen_v1.toml`. It contains 12 parallel-jaw
training profiles, five new deterministic episodes per profile, and three
matched groups:

1. `historical-controller`: historical reset and historical grasp control.
2. `collision-checked-reset`: only collision-checked reset is enabled.
3. `geometry-aware-planner`: collision-checked reset plus the complete planner.

Each group runs the same 60 randomized samples, for 180 closed-loop episodes in
total. Local episode IDs `100000`--`100004` are screening data. The separate
range `200000`--`200039` is reserved and must not be inspected until a final
method and confirmatory protocol are frozen.

Evaluation-only profiles requiring suction or a cradle are excluded. Their
absence is an end-effector capability boundary, not a failed parallel-jaw task.

## Fixed constraints

- One visible AMD Radeon GPU through ROCm and PyTorch `cuda:0`.
- Genesis physics, the checked-in catalog hash, seed 42, and 30 Hz control.
- The 35 N contact-force abort remains unchanged in every group.
- Full randomized samples and episode IDs must match before statistics run.
- No parameter is changed after viewing a screening episode and then reported
  as if it had been preregistered.
- A completed summary is accepted after Genesis cleanup exit 139 only when the
  runner verifies every frozen episode and profile.

## Outcomes and statistics

The primary screening outcome is closed-loop task success. Safety outcomes are
force aborts and drops. Secondary outcomes are maximum contact force, simulated
episode duration, successful parcels per hour, inference latency, and grasp
planning compute time.

The report generated by `scripts/analyze_campaign_results.py` includes:

- counts, rates, and Wilson 95% intervals;
- two-sided exact McNemar tests for paired success, force-abort, and drop
  outcomes;
- paired candidate-minus-reference mean effects with deterministic bootstrap
  95% intervals and sign-flip randomization tests for force and duration;
- per-profile strata and macro profile success, so a large profile cannot hide
  a cylinder regression;
- planner attempts and synchronized compute milliseconds per attempt.

This 5-per-profile campaign is an elimination screen, not a reliability claim.
Raw p-values are diagnostic and no multiplicity-corrected confirmatory claim is
made from them.

## Decision gate after screening

The geometry method advances only if all of the following hold relative to
collision-checked reset:

- aggregate successes do not decrease;
- force aborts and drops do not increase;
- no supported profile loses more than one of five matched successes;
- planning telemetry is present whenever the planner is active;
- non-active lower boxes and cylinders preserve their matched behavior, or any
  difference is explained and reproduced.

Profiles with a useful signal proceed to a 20--40 episode confirmatory stratum
from the reserved namespace. The primary confirmatory comparison will be
geometry-aware planning versus collision-checked reset. Success is primary;
force abort and drop are safety co-outcomes. Any additional hypotheses require
an explicit family and Holm correction before execution.

## Planned ablations

After screening identifies relevant profiles, run one matched change at a time:

- disable collision filtering;
- disable manipulability ranking;
- disable the symmetric wrist candidate;
- disable retry replanning;
- disable the tall-box 2.5 mm approach tier;
- optional diagnostic only: enable swept waypoint collision checking.

The last diagnostic is not a default candidate because prior measurements
showed no rejected challenge waypoint and up to 7.76 seconds of overhead per
episode.

## Method and model roadmap

The immediate method remains a deterministic geometry and safety controller.
This is deliberate: it creates a reliable expert and a measurable safety layer
for later learning. The next learning order is ACT RGB, ACT RGB-D, compact
Diffusion RGB-D, then a small VLA only for high-level task conditioning. Every
learned policy remains inside the same 35 N supervisor.

Primary research anchors, retrieved through academic APIs and recorded in
`RESEARCH_AND_MODEL_MATRIX.md`, are ACT (RSS 2023, DOI
`10.15607/RSS.2023.XIX.016`), Diffusion Policy (RSS 2023, DOI
`10.15607/RSS.2023.XIX.026`), DP3 (RSS 2024, DOI
`10.15607/RSS.2024.XX.067`), OpenVLA (arXiv `2406.09246`), and Octo (RSS 2024,
DOI `10.15607/RSS.2024.XX.090`). The current collision-aware GPU planning
direction is also compared with cuRobo (arXiv `2310.17274`), while the project
keeps its implementation independent of CUDA-only code.

## Reproduction

On the Radeon instance:

```bash
cd /workspace/parcel-sorter-opt-v1
bash scripts/run_geometry_screen_campaign_rocm.sh
```

The runner validates the campaign fingerprint, refuses an existing output
directory, executes groups sequentially on one GPU, verifies complete summaries,
builds compact evidence, failure analyses, matched comparisons, a paper
evaluation JSON, and SHA-256 manifests.
