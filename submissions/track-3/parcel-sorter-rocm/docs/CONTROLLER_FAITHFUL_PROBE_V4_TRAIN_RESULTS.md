# Controller-Faithful Probe V4: Train-Only Result

## Claim Boundary

This is a positive safety-feasibility result on the frozen v2 train
population. It is not an independent development or holdout result, does not
increase task successes, and does not authorize deployment or new physics.
Its only promotion is permission to write and freeze a separate, disjoint
population protocol for the selected mechanism.

## Result

The static train baseline produced 7/32 successes and 17/32 force aborts.

| Policy | Successes | Force aborts | Safe abstentions | Changed choices | Selector P95 | Gate |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| `veto-static` | 7 | 13 | 4 | 5 | 0.006 ms | Pass |
| `force-first` | 6 | 12 | 4 | 26 | 0.007 ms | Reject: aggregate/profile/fold regressions |
| `stability-first` | 5 | 11 | 4 | 16 | 0.008 ms | Reject: aggregate/profile/fold success regressions |
| `support-first` | 7 | 12 | 4 | 31 | 0.008 ms | Reject: profile/fold success regressions |

`veto-static` preserved all seven baseline successes and reduced force aborts
by four without any safety or success regression in a profile or fold. The
four reductions were safe abstentions on one `medium_carton` and three
`near_limit_box` groups whose static choices later force-aborted. Its fifth
change selected the only eligible candidate for one `large_narrow_carton`;
both the selected and baseline candidates were safe task failures.

The active ranking variants provide an important negative control. Lower
probe force, lower drift, or stronger early contact support did not by
themselves predict full transport success. They frequently exchanged gains
and losses across groups and therefore failed the frozen regression gates.

## Decision

The fixed selector returned:

```text
status=probe_candidate_found
selected=veto-static
new_physics_authorized=false
holdout_opened=false
```

The selected mechanism is a dynamic safety veto, not a new success ranker.
The current runtime remains the static geometry baseline. The next admissible
step is to freeze a fully disjoint, broader parcel population and evaluate
`veto-static` once against the static baseline. V2 development and holdout
remain closed and cannot be used for this confirmation.
